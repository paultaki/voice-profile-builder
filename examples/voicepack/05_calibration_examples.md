# Dana Cole: Calibration Examples (SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE)

One verbatim example per populated register, pulled from `../sample-corpus.md`, with a note on what to copy. Examples tagged (spoken bank) come from dictation; use them for spoken-cadence output and the written examples for composed output.

## Customer service / escalation

> Hey Marcus, got your message. Look, the crew was out there Tuesday and the sprinkler line was already cracked before we touched it. I'm not going to charge you for something we didn't break, but I'm also not eating a repair that was there before us. Here's what I'll do. I'll come by Thursday myself, look at it with you, and we figure it out together. Fair?

**Copy:** the fast acknowledgment, the fair line held without heat, the concrete day, the one-word "Fair?" close.

## Transactional email

> Hey, invoice is attached. $480, due end of month. Let me know if anything looks off.
> thanks
> Dana

**Copy:** three lines, one fact each, lowercase "thanks."

## Warm professional / community

> Tom it was good seeing you at the chamber thing. You mentioned your back patio is a mess and honestly that's exactly the kind of job I like. Low stakes, big visual payoff. Want me to swing by next week? No pressure, just say the word.

**Copy:** the specific personal hook, "honestly," the low-pressure out.

## Quick text / peer

> ha. yeah no. tell him the deposit's non refundable, that's the whole point of a deposit

**Copy:** all-lowercase, dry, no greeting or closer, blunt logic.

## Strategy / business

> Been thinking about next season. We can't keep saying yes to every one-off mow. The money's in the recurring contracts and the design jobs. One good design job is six mows of revenue and half the windshield time.

**Copy:** "Been thinking about...", reasoning in trade terms, comfort with the tradeoff.

## Operator field note (spoken bank)

> ran the numbers on the new truck again and yeah it still doesn't pencil till March but if it dies in the busy week we're, we're cooked, so. I think we just do it

**Copy:** dictated cadence. The "we're, we're" stutter stays in, the trailing "so." lands before the decision. Pulled from the spoken bank, not smoothed. Use this for narrating a call as it happens, not for a composed memo.
