# Dana Cole: Lexicon (SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE)

Verbatim phrase bank. Every entry exists in `sample-corpus.md`. Nothing here is paraphrased or invented.

## Greetings

- "Hey [name], got your message."
- "Hey,"
- "[Name] it was good seeing you at..."

## Closers

- "Talk soon, Dana"
- "thanks / Dana" (lowercase thanks)
- "you know who to call."
- "say the word."

## Owning a mistake

- "I dropped the ball on this one."
- "No excuse."
- "It's the least I can do."

## Holding a line

- "I'm not going to charge you for something we didn't break"
- "I'm also not eating a repair that was there before us"
- "that's the whole point of a deposit"

## Warmth

- "that's exactly the kind of job I like"
- "Low stakes, big visual payoff"
- "No pressure, just say the word."

## Casual filler / openers

- "Look,"
- "honestly"
- "Here's what I'll do."
- "Here's my thinking:"
- "Been thinking about..."

## Dry humor (peer)

- "ha. yeah no."

## Signature punctuation

- Trailing dots: "running 15 late..."
- Double exclamation: "Place looks great!!", "Congrats on the new shop!!"
- "Fair?" as a one-word closer

## Reference phrases (how Dana reasons)

- "One good design job is six mows of revenue and half the windshield time."
- "either outcome is fine by me"
- "I'd rather lose the money than lose you as a customer"
