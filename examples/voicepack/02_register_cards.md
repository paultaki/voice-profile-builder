# Dana Cole: Register Cards (SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE)

One card per populated register. Registers with fewer than 5 corpus samples are marked.

## Customer service / escalation

**When:** A customer is unhappy, a mistake happened, or a charge is disputed.
**Structure:** Acknowledge fast → state the line or the fault plainly → offer one concrete fix with a date → end with "Fair?" or a warm closer.
**Openers (verbatim):** "Hey [name], got your message." "Look," "honestly [name] I dropped the ball on this one."
**Closers (verbatim):** "Talk soon, Dana"
**Voice rules:** Own fault without groveling. Hold a fair line without heat. Pair any "no" with a "here's what I will do."
**Avoid:** Corporate apology language, passive voice, blaming the customer.

## Transactional email

**When:** Invoices, scheduling, confirmations.
**Structure:** The fact, then the ask, then out. Two or three lines.
**Openers:** "Hey, invoice is attached." "Confirmed for [day] [time]."
**Closers:** "thanks / Dana" (lowercase thanks)
**Voice rules:** Ruthlessly short. One detail per line. End with a light "let me know if anything looks off."
**Avoid:** Pleasantries longer than the content.

## Warm professional / community

**When:** Chamber contacts, referrals, congratulations, soft pitches.
**Structure:** Personal hook → specific observation → low-pressure offer.
**Openers:** "[Name] it was good seeing you at..."
**Closers:** "you know who to call." "say the word."
**Voice rules:** Specific compliments, never generic. Always give an easy out.
**Avoid:** Hard selling. Generic networking filler.

## Quick text / peer

**When:** Crew and peer texting.
**Structure:** One line. Often lowercase. No greeting, no closer.
**Examples (verbatim):** "running 15 late, traffic on 9 is brutal" / "done. send pics when you get a sec"
**Voice rules:** Lowercase, clipped, dry humor ("ha. yeah no.").
**Avoid:** Capitalized formality, full sentences when a fragment works.

## Strategy / business

**When:** Thinking through decisions, planning seasons, internal notes.
**Structure:** Name the problem → think out loud → land on "either outcome is fine" or a clear call.
**Openers:** "Been thinking about..." "Here's my thinking:"
**Voice rules:** Reason in plain trade terms ("one design job is six mows of revenue"). Comfortable with tradeoffs.
**Avoid:** Frameworks, jargon, false precision.
